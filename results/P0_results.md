# Static-weight baseline and sensitivity analysis

`P0_results.json` contains the static-weight baseline, threshold sensitivity,
and band-share results for the six datasets on which the static-weight
baseline was computed: Exchange Rate, ETTh1, ETTh2, ETTm1, ETTm2, and Weather.

Electricity is not included in the static-weight baseline; the Electricity
comparison is reported in the manuscript.
